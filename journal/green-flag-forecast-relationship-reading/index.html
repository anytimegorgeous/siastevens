Green Flag Forecast: The Crystal Ball That Reads Your Relationship

A crystal ball that doesn’t read the stars. It reads your answers. Two minutes, free, and you walk away with a score out of 10, what’s in the way, and what to do about it. Online any time, or in person when Sia brings it to your event.

By Sia StevensNovember 6, 20265 min read

If you have ever typed relationship forecast into Google, you know what comes back: horoscopes. Venus is in retrograde, Thursday looks romantic, and nothing you could actually do about any of it.

The Green Flag Forecast is the opposite. It’s a crystal ball that doesn’t read the stars. It reads your answers. It takes about two minutes, it’s free, and at the end the ball tells you three things: your chances out of 10, what’s in the way, and what to do about it.

How the reading works

First you tell the ball where you are: single, seeing someone, or in a relationship. Each one gets its own reading. Then it asks a few quick questions about real life, the kind of thing a fortune teller would guess by watching you: what your evenings look like, how much of your day goes to your phone, what the two of you actually do together. Every tap gets an answer from the ball, and every answer decides the next question, so no two readings run the same.

The Green Flag Forecast is AI-powered, and it is built on the framework from A Million Green Flags: love grows in three stages, attraction, reciprocation and co-creation, and most relationship trouble is one of those stages stalling. The ball’s job is to find which one.

What it looks for

  • If you’re single: whether your drive is being drained before it reaches a real person (endless scrolling, swiping and screens), whether you know what you actually like in someone, whether you’re looking in the right places, and whether you’re building the kind of life someone would want to join. The number is your chances of finding someone.
  • If you’re seeing someone: whether it’s attraction alone or you genuinely relate, whether you’re sharing and doing things together, and whether your lives are starting to lean on each other. The number is your chances of this going long term.
  • If you’re in a relationship: whether you’ve built things together, and whether you’re still building. The number is your chances of lasting.

The result lists everything the ball found, not just the score, because a number on its own tells you nothing you can use. The point is the next step.

A live reading at your event

The Forecast started at a booth, and that’s still where it’s most fun. Sia Stevens brings the crystal ball to events in person and guides each guest through their reading herself. It takes about two minutes a person, it’s free for guests, and everyone walks away with their reading printed out, like the card from a Zoltar fortune-teller machine, with a QR code back to the full framework.

It works anywhere people are curious about love:

  • Dating and singles events: speed dating, singles mixers, matchmaking nights and dating club events, where it doubles as an icebreaker. Two strangers comparing their readings already have something to talk about.
  • Markets, fairs and festivals: experience markets and night markets with tarot readers, games and activities, where a crystal ball booth draws a line.
  • Pop-ups and community events: book events, wellness days, workplace and campus events, private parties.

If you’re an organizer looking for pop-up vendors, interactive booth ideas, singles event activities, or a modern alternative to a fortune teller for your party, this is built for you: quick, memorable, and grounded in an actual framework instead of the stars.
