Sia Stevens — writer, relationship coach, and self-described recovering software engineer — is the it girl of self-help this season, and her new release A Million Green Flags is already living rent-free in our group chat. 🚩💚

The Pitch

Forget your red flag checklist, bestie. Stevens says the real tea is green flags — and she's got receipts (John Gottman! Harvard! Actual footnotes!) to prove your situationship isn't "just fading," it's a documented crime scene.

The Juiciest Bits

The "frosting vs. cake" theory is about to end a thousand talking stages. The "Tuesday Test" says anyone can show up for a Saturday date — but can they show up for a random Tuesday? And one gut-punch chapter reminds us Steve Jobs wouldn't even give his own kids an iPad. 📵

The Drama

Stevens isn't afraid to go there on the "two pulls" chapter — gender, evolution, receipts on both sides — bold, but she keeps it classy. No shade thrown either direction.

Our Only Gripe

Every. Single. Chapter. Ends. The. Same. Way. ("The Practice," "The Green Flag" — we get it, Sia, we're taking notes, we promise!)

The Bottom Line

Ditch the group chat advice. This one's getting the highlighter treatment. 💛
