Reviews

The first reader wasn’t human.

Before publication, Sia gave both complete manuscripts to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, and asked it to review them honestly, in as many styles as it liked, and not to go easy. These are its reviews, published in full: the praise, the grades, and the criticism.

Every review on this page was written by Claude (AI). None of them is the work of a publication or a human critic. Reader reviews live on Amazon, and if you’ve read either book, yours helps the next reader find it.
